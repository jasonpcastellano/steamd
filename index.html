About Steamdle.io

Steamdle.io is an interactive higher-or-lower web game inspired by Steam concurrent player statistics.

How to Play:

  • Two games are displayed on screen.
  • The left game shows its current active concurrent players.
  • Guess if the right game has HIGHER or LOWER active players.
  • Get it right to advance your streak! One wrong answer ends the run.

Player estimates are based on recent peak concurrent player snapshots across popular titles on Steam.
